Havasupai Falls

Turquoise waterfalls deep in the Grand Canyon. A 4-day backpacking trip on Havasupai tribal land — permits required.

📍 Supai, Arizona 🥾 20 mi round-trip 🗓️ Dates TBD

Itinerary

4 Days, 3 Nights

Day 1 Hike In

Hualapai Hilltop → Campground

Start before sunrise (5–6am) to beat the heat. 8 mi descent through the canyon to Supai Village, then 2 mi more to the campground between Havasu and Mooney Falls. Set up camp, cool off in Havasu Falls.

Day 2 Beaver Falls

Mooney + Beaver Falls Day Hike

Climb down the chains/ladders past Mooney Falls (200 ft, the tallest), then 3 mi downstream to Beaver Falls — multi-tiered turquoise pools perfect for swimming. ~8 mi round-trip from camp.

Day 3 Explore + Rest

Navajo + Fifty Foot Falls

Easy day. Visit Navajo Falls and Fifty Foot Falls (upstream of camp). Swim, relax, journal. Confirm Day 4 plan — pack lightly for the climb out.

Day 4 Hike Out

Campground → Hualapai Hilltop

Leave camp by 4–5am. The last 1.5 mi is steep switchbacks back to the rim — save energy. Drive to Las Vegas or Flagstaff for a real bed and a real meal.

What to pack

Gear Checklist

Essentials

  • Backpack (50–65L)
  • Tent + sleeping pad
  • Sleeping bag (30°F)
  • Water filter / Sawyer
  • Hiking boots (broken in)
  • Trekking poles
  • Headlamp + batteries
  • Sun hat + sunscreen
  • Swimwear + quick-dry towel
  • Water shoes (for falls)
  • Permit printout + ID
  • Cash (no cards in village)

Food (4 days, no fires allowed)

Plan ~2,500 cal/day. Dehydrated meals, oatmeal, trail mix, jerky, electrolyte tabs. Café in Supai Village sells fry bread + basics — cash only.

Getting there

Logistics

Fly to Las Vegas (LAS)

Closest major airport. ~4 hr drive to Hualapai Hilltop. Rent a car with high clearance — last 60 miles are remote, no gas stations near trailhead.

Night before: Peach Springs or Kingman, AZ

Sleep close to the trailhead. Peach Springs is ~1 hr from Hualapai Hilltop. Hualapai Lodge is the closest option.
